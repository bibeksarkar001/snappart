    Why the mat matters

    Each code is exactly 22 mm. Seeing six or more in a frame tells SnapPart where the camera was and how big a millimetre is, with no special hardware.

    About

    Film a part.
    Print a copy.

    A part you can't buy shouldn't be the end of the thing it belongs to. A cracked latch on a toolbox, a snapped bracket under a favourite toy car, a hinge nobody sells anymore. SnapPart lets you film the piece on a printed mat and get a measured 3D model you can print.

    How it works

    Film. Print the mat, tape it flat, film a high orbit, a low orbit and the part flipped. Build. The codes give each frame its camera position and scale, SAM 3.1 outlines the part in every frame, and the outlines carve the shape. Review and print. Muse Spark asks for what a camera can't see; answer by typing, tapping or speaking with Muse Voice, then download the STL.

    Honest about accuracy

    On our test part (51.6 × 41.9 × 11.8 mm by calipers), overall sizes came within 0.1 to 0.26 mm and the four slots measured 7.43 × 2.54 mm against 7.4 × 2.6 mm. The held-out score of 0.95 says how well the model matches 10 frames it was never built from. Your results depend on light, video and how flat the mat lies. Method and numbers

    Built with open source

    OpenCV, NumPy, SciPy, scikit-image, Open3D, trimesh, FastAPI, React and three.js, among others. All, with licences, in the README

    What we collect

    • Frames your browser pulls from your videos (not the full videos).
    • Voice recordings, only if you speak, only to make text.
    • What you type: part name, description, answers, corrections.
    • What we make: model, report, question cards, review images.
    • IP address and request times, only to prevent abuse.

    No name, email or account.

    How it's processed

    Frames, text and voice go to Meta's Model API (SAM 3.1, Muse Spark 1.3, Muse Voice Transcribe) on the standard endpoints, not the "contributor" ones under which content may be used for training. Site on Vercel, build server on [backend host]. We never sell data or use it for ads.

    How long we keep it

    Voice: deleted right after transcription. Frames, model, report and answers: 7 days, then deleted. Delete your part anytime from its Result page.

    Who can see your part

    Anyone with your private link or code. We don't list or publish parts. Contact [contact email].
