Reference object

A photo has no size on its own. A card or coin of known size next to the part turns pixels into millimetres.

A bank card is 85.60 × 53.98 mm everywhere and gives the most accurate scale. We check what we find with you before measuring.

After the photos, Muse Spark looks up the coin on the web and asks you to confirm its diameter before we measure.
