SnapPart
Workspace About Roadmap

A photo in.
A printable part out.

SnapPart turns photos of a simple flat or round part into a 3D file in millimetres, so a broken bracket or a lost wheel can be printed again.

The problem

Small plastic parts break and replacements are hard to find. Modelling one from scratch in CAD takes skills and time most people do not have. A phone camera is already in every pocket.

How it works

1

Guided photos

Top and front views next to a bank card or coin of known size.

2

Measure

The part is cut out of each photo, and sizes come from pixels and the reference.

3

Build

A model writes a CAD script. It runs in a sandbox and exports an STL.

Which model does what

Muse Spark 1.3 Checks the photos, identifies a coin with search and your confirmation, and writes the CAD script.
SAM 3.1 Cuts the part, the reference and any hand out of each photo.
Muse Wonder 1.0 Steps in to repair the script when the first attempts fail.
No AI Measuring from pixels with OpenCV, and building the solid with CadQuery.

Limits, said plainly

  • Simple flat and round parts only.
  • Shiny, clear or very dark parts photograph badly.
  • Hidden details are not guessed. Add more views.
  • Design mode handles simple shapes and short names only.
